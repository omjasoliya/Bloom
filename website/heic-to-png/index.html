How to convert HEIC to PNG on Android

  1. Install Bloom (Android 10 or newer) from the download page.
  2. Select one or more photos in your Gallery and tap Share → Bloom.
  3. On the Convert tab, tap PNG (or JPG, WEBP or PDF).
  4. Find the new files in Downloads/Bloom, or tap Share to send them straight away.

On Windows or Linux

  1. Start dragging the HEIC photo (or a selection of photos).
  2. Press Shift mid-drag and drop on PNG (or JPG).
  3. The converted photo appears next to the original.

Why convert HEIC?

HEIC (also called HEIF) is the format iPhones use for photos. It saves space, but many websites, forms, Windows apps and older programs can't open it. PNG and JPG open everywhere.

  • Upload to websites & forms
  • Open on Windows
  • Edit in any app
  • Share with anyone

Questions

How do I convert HEIC to PNG?

Share the HEIC photo to Bloom and tap PNG. On Windows and Linux, drag the photo, press Shift and drop it on PNG. The PNG is saved next to the original.

Is the conversion done offline?

Yes. Bloom converts the photo on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I convert HEIC to JPG instead?

Yes. Tap JPG instead of PNG. JPG is smaller; PNG keeps every detail and transparency.

Can I convert many HEIC photos at once?

Yes. Select all of them, share them to Bloom and pick a format. Every photo gets its own file.

Is there a watermark or limit?

No. Bloom is free and open source, with no watermark, no file limit and no account.

Which devices does it support?

Android 10 and newer, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux (Intel/AMD and ARM64).
