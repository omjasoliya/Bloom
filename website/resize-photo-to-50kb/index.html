Photo size for forms

Resize your photo to under 50 KB, without uploading it

Exam, job and KYC forms want a photo under 50 KB, but your phone's photo is 3 MB. Bloom shrinks it to just under the limit in about a second, right on your device.

Download for Android, Windows or LinuxWhat is Bloom?

How to resize a photo to 50 KB on Android

  1. Install Bloom (Android 10 or newer) from the download page.
  2. Open the photo in your Gallery or Files app and tap Share → Bloom.
  3. Open the Tools tab and tap Fit size.
  4. Pick 50 KB (or 20, 100, 200 or 500 KB). The new JPG is saved in Downloads/Bloom, just under the limit.

On Windows or Linux

  1. Install Bloom and start dragging the photo.
  2. Press Ctrl mid-drag and drop it on Fit size.
  3. Choose the size. The result appears next to the original, which is never changed.

Exact size like 200 × 230 pixels?

Many forms ask for both a file size and exact dimensions, for example JPG, 10–50 KB, 200 × 230 px. In Fit size, choose Custom, type the KB limit, the width and the height. Bloom crops from the centre to that exact size and finds the best quality that stays under the limit.

Why not an online resizer?

Your photo stays with you

Online resizers upload your photo, and often your signature and ID too, to a server you know nothing about. Bloom does the work on your device.

Best quality for the size

Bloom keeps as many pixels and as much quality as fit, so a 3 MB photo becomes about 49 KB that still looks sharp.

Works offline

Questions

How do I reduce a photo to under 50 KB without losing quality?

Use Bloom's Fit size tool and pick 50 KB. It searches for the highest JPEG quality and the most pixels that still fit, so the result lands just under 50 KB and stays sharp.

Can I resize a signature to under 20 KB?

Yes. Share the signature image to Bloom, tap Tools, then Fit size, then 20 KB.

My form needs exactly 200 × 230 pixels. Can Bloom do that?

Yes. Choose Custom in Fit size and enter the KB limit plus the width and height. Bloom crops from the centre to that exact size.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Bloom resizes the photo on your own phone or computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Is Bloom free?

Yes. Bloom is free and open source, with no ads and no account.
