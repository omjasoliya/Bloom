Download for Android, Windows or LinuxWhat is Bloom?

How to convert JPG to PDF on Android

  1. Install Bloom (Android 10 or newer) from the download page.
  2. Select one or more photos in your Gallery and tap Share → Bloom.
  3. On the Convert tab, tap PDF. Several photos become one PDF, one page each.
  4. Find the PDF in Downloads/Bloom, or tap Share to send it straight away.

On Windows or Linux

  1. Start dragging the image (or a selection of images).
  2. Press Shift mid-drag and drop on PDF. For several images in one file, press Ctrl and drop on To PDF.
  3. The PDF appears next to your images.

Also works the other way

Share a PDF to Bloom and tap JPG or PNG to turn every page into an image. Bloom can also merge several PDFs into one and split a PDF into pages.

Why convert offline?

Private

Documents and IDs never leave your device. Online converters keep a copy on their servers.

Fast

No upload, no waiting in a queue. A PDF is ready in about a second.

Free

No watermark, no page limit, no account. Bloom is open source.

Questions

How do I convert several JPGs into one PDF?

Select all the photos, share them to Bloom and tap PDF. Bloom makes a single PDF with one page per photo.

Is the PDF made offline?

Yes. Bloom builds the PDF on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.

Is there a watermark or page limit?

No. Bloom is free and open source, with no watermark, no page limit and no account.

Can I turn a PDF back into images?

Yes. Share the PDF to Bloom and tap JPG or PNG to save every page as an image.

Which devices does it support?

Android 10 and newer, Windows 10 and 11, and Linux (Intel/AMD and ARM64).
